# Licensing

The project is distributed under the [MIT License](../../LICENSE).

The project is a reusable agent skill: instructions, references, templates,
and a small validation script. A short permissive license fits its intended
distribution. Retain the complete license with distributed copies: the root LICENSE and the
identical copy in `skills/stacks/LICENSE`, which travels with the installed
package.

The terms permit reuse, modification, redistribution, and commercial use
while requiring preservation of the copyright and permission notice. They
include a warranty disclaimer and do not require adaptations to be published
under the same license. The license text governs these permissions.

## Maintaining the license

Keep the complete license with distributed copies and preserve required
notices. Changes to licensing terms require an explicit owner decision; do
not infer one from an editorial or technical maintenance request. Record an
approved change in the [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md), then validate
that the README, frontmatter, root license, and package license copy remain
consistent.

Technology names in the references identify independent projects; their
licenses are not changed by this skill. Verify dependency licenses when
recommending an application stack. Preserve required notices if future
contributions incorporate third-party text or code, and identify its source
and scope rather than assuming this project's license covers it.
